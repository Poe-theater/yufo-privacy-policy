Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how personal data is handled in relation to YUFO ("the Game"), developed with the Unity engine.

2. No Collection of Personal Data

YUFO does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from its users. The Game:

  • does not require registration or an account;
  • does not contain advertising;
  • does not use analytics or tracking tools;
  • does not connect to external servers to send user or device data;
  • does not share any information with third parties.

3. Locally Stored Data

Game progress (scores, levels, settings) is saved exclusively locally on the user's device and is never transmitted elsewhere. Uninstalling the app automatically deletes this data.

4. Game Engine (Unity)

The Game is built with the Unity engine. In its current configuration, no Unity services that collect data are enabled (e.g. Unity Analytics, Unity Ads). Should third-party services be integrated in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly before the release of the new version.

5. Children's Data

Since the Game does not collect any personal data, it can be safely used by minors as well, with no privacy-related risks.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in case of changes to how the Game operates (for example, if advertising or analytics services are introduced in the future). The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at: poetheaters@gmail.com
