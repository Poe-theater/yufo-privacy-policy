Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how personal data is handled in relation to YUFO: Merge to Space ("the Game"), developed with the Unity engine.

2. Data Collection

The Game does not require registration or a user account. However, the Game:

  • displays advertising through third-party ad networks (Unity Ads and AppLovin);
  • uses these networks' SDKs, which may collect certain device and usage data for the purpose of showing and measuring ads;
  • allows the user to optionally enter a nickname to appear on a local, offline leaderboard, stored only on the device.

The Game does not collect or store the user's real name, email address, phone number, or other directly identifying information.

3. Data Collected via Advertising SDKs

The Game integrates the following third-party advertising and mediation SDKs:

  • Unity Ads
  • AppLovin (MAX)

These SDKs may collect and share the following categories of data for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes:

  • Approximate location
  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • App diagnostics and performance data
  • App interaction data (ad impressions, clicks)

This data is transmitted to Unity Technologies and AppLovin Corp., and possibly to their advertising partners, in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Ads Privacy Policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • AppLovin Privacy Policy: applovin.com/privacy

Users can opt out of personalized advertising and reset their Advertising ID through their device settings (Android Settings > Google > Ads).

4. Locally Stored Data

Game progress (scores, levels, settings) and the optional leaderboard nickname are saved exclusively locally on the user's device and are never transmitted to our own servers. Uninstalling the app automatically deletes this locally stored data.

5. Game Engine (Unity)

The Game is built with the Unity engine and uses the Unity Ads service as described above. Should additional third-party services be integrated in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly before the release of the new version.

6. Children's Data

This Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13. The advertising SDKs used may not be configured for child-directed treatment; therefore, use by children under 13 is not recommended in this version of the Game.

7. Data Retention

Locally stored data (progress, settings, nickname) is retained on the device until the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared by the user. Data collected by third-party advertising SDKs is retained according to each provider's own privacy policy and retention practices.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR in the EU/EEA, CCPA in California), you may have rights regarding data collected by third-party advertising SDKs, including the right to access, delete, or opt out of the sale/sharing of your data. Please refer to the privacy policies of Unity Ads and AppLovin linked above to exercise these rights directly with them.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in case of changes to how the Game operates (for example, if additional advertising or analytics services are introduced in the future). The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at: poetheaters@gmail.com
