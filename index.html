Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how personal data is handled in relation to YUFO: Merge to Space ("the Game"), a mobile game developed with the Unity engine.

The Game is published by Poe theater, based in Italy, which is the data controller for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact details are in section 11.

2. Data Collection

The Game does not require registration or a user account. However, the Game:

  • displays advertising through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and Unity Ads;
  • uses these services' SDKs, which may collect certain device and usage data for the purpose of showing and measuring ads;
  • allows the user to optionally enter a nickname to appear on a local, offline leaderboard, stored only on the device.

The Game does not collect or store the user's real name, email address, phone number, or other directly identifying information.

3. Data Collected via Advertising SDKs

The Game integrates the following third-party advertising and mediation SDKs, both operated by Unity Technologies:

  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) – ad mediation
  • Unity Ads – ad network

These SDKs may collect and share the following categories of data for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes:

  • Approximate location (derived from the IP address)
  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • App diagnostics and performance data
  • App interaction data (ad impressions, clicks)

This data is transmitted to Unity Technologies, and possibly to its advertising partners, in accordance with its own privacy policy:

  • Unity Privacy Policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Data collected by the advertising SDKs may be processed outside the EU/EEA, including in the United States.

4. Your Consent and Advertising Choices

On first launch the Game asks whether you agree to personalized ads. If you decline, ads are still shown but they are not personalized. The Game does not start the advertising SDKs until you have made a choice.

Personalized advertising is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw or change it at any time from Settings > Privacy inside the Game. Your choice is stored only on your device.

You can also opt out of personalized advertising and reset or delete your Advertising ID through your device settings (Android Settings > Google > Ads).

5. Locally Stored Data

Game progress (scores and the current run), settings, your advertising choice and the optional leaderboard nickname are saved exclusively locally on the user's device and are never transmitted to our own servers. Uninstalling the app automatically deletes this locally stored data.

6. Third-Party Services

The Game is built with the Unity engine and uses the advertising services described above. It uses no other analytics or tracking services. Should additional third-party services be integrated in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly before the release of the new version.

7. Children's Data

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

8. Data Retention

Locally stored data (progress, settings, advertising choice, nickname) is retained on the device until the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared by the user. Data collected by the advertising SDKs is retained according to the provider's own privacy policy and retention practices.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR in the EU/EEA, CCPA in California), you may have the right to:

  • access the data held about you and obtain a copy of it;
  • have it corrected or deleted;
  • withdraw your consent to personalized advertising at any time (see section 4);
  • object to or restrict the processing, or opt out of the sale/sharing of your data;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

We do not hold any of your data on our own servers. To exercise these rights over data collected by the advertising SDKs, please refer to the Unity privacy policy linked in section 3, or contact us and we will help you direct your request.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in case of changes to how the Game operates (for example, if additional advertising or analytics services are introduced in the future). The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at: poetheaters@gmail.com
